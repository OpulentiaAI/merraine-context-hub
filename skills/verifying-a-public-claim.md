# Verifying a public claim

Read [[facts]] and [[cite-what-you-read]].

Search narrow. Read the page. Never cite a snippet. Confidence is high
(two independent sources), medium (one solid), or low (thin). Unverified
goes in Gaps. Do not write the piece.
