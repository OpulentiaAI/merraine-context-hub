# Editing the draft

One pass, one question. Do not hunt for everything at once.

1. Sense. Two ideas in one sentence, a pronoun with no referent.
2. Voice. Does it still sound like [[jeremy-voice]]?
3. Payoff. What does the reader get from this line?
4. Proof. Number, source, named example, or an explicit hedge.
5. Specific. If you cannot name it, drop the claim.
