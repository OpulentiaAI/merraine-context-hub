# Merraine ICP

## Positioning

Merraine places senior operators and executives at companies that are scaling or
rebuilding a leadership bench. The buyer is whoever owns the open seat: a CEO who
just raised, a COO absorbing a departure, a CHRO with three senior reqs open for a
month. The status quo we displace is internal recruiting plus contingency firms
plus "post it and see." We win on speed to a real shortlist.
