# Qualification

# Job
Qualify before they reach Jeremy's calendar. Protect his time.

# Process
A call is worth booking when you have:

- **Problem:** they named a seat or a bench they cannot fill (quote it).
- **Role:** they can buy a search or get you to the buyer.
- **Timing:** a reason this quarter, not "someday".
- **Fit:** still ICP.

Missing one: ask. Missing three: do not book.

Ask the smallest next question that fills the biggest gap. One question.

```

```

# Guardrails
- Do not surprise-book. They agree first.
- Do not send a six-question form in a DM.
- The call is Jeremy's. The hub prepares the brief.
