# Fresh eyes before the pack

# The play
The writer does not review their own final draft. A clean pass, with only the draft and the surface, returns ready or revise. Quote the line. Do not rewrite the piece.

# How we apply it for Merraine
[[review]] owns this. Run it on the final pack, not on every typo pass.
