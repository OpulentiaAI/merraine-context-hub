# The five Sales Navigator filters that matter

## The play

Sales Navigator is roughly $99/month and most people use a fraction of it.

1. **Posted on LinkedIn in past 30 days.** Active users are active buyers. Someone
   who has not posted in six months is not reading their inbox either.
2. **Changed jobs in past 90 days.** New VPs have budget and something to prove.
   The author's opener: *"congrats on the new role. most new [title]s we work with
   are focused on [common goal]. worth a quick chat?"*
3. **Company headcount growth**, filtered to 20%+ over the past year. Growing means
   hiring means budget. Shrinking means frozen.
4. **Account lists plus alerts.** Save the top 200 targets and get notified when
   anyone posts, changes role, or the company makes news.
5. **Boolean search**, e.g. `(VP OR "Vice President" OR Director) AND (Sales OR
   Revenue OR Growth) NOT (HR OR Recruiting)`.

## How we apply it for Merraine

**Ask #2 is a purchase, not a build.** Do not spend an engineering hour replicating
Sales Navigator. Buy the seat, save the top 200 as an account list, and let its
alerts feed [[executive-move]].

One inversion for a search firm: his boolean should *include* HR and Recruiting
titles, since CHRO and Head of Talent are buyers here, not noise.

Filter 2 is the highest-value one for Merraine and maps directly to
[[executive-move]]: a new COO rebuilds their bench within two quarters.
