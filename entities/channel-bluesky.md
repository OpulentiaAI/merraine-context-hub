# Bluesky

# Shape
Candid and specific. The room is allergic to marketing speak. Link the source. Alt text on every image. See [[writing-for-bluesky]].

# Limits
- About 300 graphemes.
- One or two topical tags, never a wall.
- Not connected. Do not invent a handle.

# Failures
Polished promo. Buried links. Images with no alt text.
