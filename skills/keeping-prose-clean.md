# Keeping prose clean

Front-load. Concrete over abstract. Match [[jeremy-voice]], not a default.

Instant cuts: em dashes, delve, leverage, utilise, "it's not just X, it's Y",
"in today's [anything]", "I hope this finds you well".
Then [[slop-patterns]]. Two trips means rewrite.
