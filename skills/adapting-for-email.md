# Adapting for email

Read [[email]] and [[email-pointer-vs-whole]]. Decide the job, then cut.

A page can build an argument. Mail has to earn the next second.
Pointer or whole thing. Never two thirds of a page plus "read the rest".
Fill [[email-components]]. Do not harden a hedge. `sendReady: no`.
