# Threads

# Shape
Talk like a person. One idea. A short observation or a real question on line one, then one beat, then a light landing. Write to earn a reply, never to beg for one. See [[writing-for-threads]].

# Limits
- Cap 500 characters. You rarely need all of it.
- One topic tag at most. The tag may be a multi-word phrase.
- Native media over a pasted link. Short video under about 60 seconds when it carries the point.
- Not connected. Do not invent a queue or a handle.

# Failures
Corporate announcement-speak. "Agree?". A pasted X post. A pitch.
