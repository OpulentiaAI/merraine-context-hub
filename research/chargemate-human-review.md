# The agent finds. The human chooses.

# The play
New leads are surfaced for a human review. He decides who is worth
contacting, edits the line, and lets the tool handle connections, timing,
and follow-ups. Static list uploads of the same ICP, with no timely
signal, hit worse. Signal plus review beat a bigger Apollo list.

# How we apply it for Merraine
This is already how this hub is built: draft-then-wait, confirm word
`send`, ChargeMate's shape without their product. Jeremy reviews the digest
and the pack. The automation never chooses the send.
