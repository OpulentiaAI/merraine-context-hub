# Settle what we beat before we write

# The play
Positioning is a decision about the market and the alternative, and
the words come last. The real alternative is often internal recruiting or
a contingency firm, not another search firm. A differentiator must be
true, provable, and hard to copy. "We care" and "we are fast" fail.

# How we apply it for Merraine
Use [[settling-positioning]] before any new sequence or site line.
Proof we may cite is still a `[NEED]` on [[icp-context]]. Do not write
around that gap.
