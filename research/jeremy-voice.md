# Jeremy Sanchez voice

# The play
Write the way Jeremy already writes.

- Open on the other person's name.
- One specific beat. Then stop adding color.
- Short lines. Concrete numbers.
- One ask. Close on a question.
- Sign off **Best, Jeremy**. Cheers is fine when the thread is already warm.
- Do not use em dashes.
- Faith, family, and Chicago can be named when they are true. Do not decorate
  with them.

Recurring marks to keep:

- **LDW** for Labor Day weekend
- **411:** when handing the team context
- **Flowers to X** for public credit
- **Salamat Po** with Filipino colleagues
- Censored `$h!t` when quoting the Lazerows' book

Spell **Lazerow** and **Farmers Fridge** correctly even if a draft slipped.
