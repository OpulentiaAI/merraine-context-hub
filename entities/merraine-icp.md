# Merraine ICP

## Positioning

Merraine places senior operators and executives at companies that are scaling or
rebuilding a leadership bench. The buyer is whoever owns the open seat: a CEO
who just raised, a COO absorbing a departure, a CHRO with three senior reqs
open for a month. The status quo we displace is internal recruiting plus
contingency firms plus "post it and see." We win on speed to a real shortlist.

Priority markets for the daily scan: Chicago, Boston, New York, Ohio, Florida.
Other US markets only when the signal is standout. The daily scan itself is
[[daily-hiring-scan]].

The keyword thesis already running in Spear — funding stage plus seniority —
is in [[spear-signal-vocabulary]]. Treat it as how you already search, not as
a confirmed industry list.
