# Newsletter

# Job
Write one issue to one person. Not a table of contents. Not the daily
hiring scan.

# Process
1. Read [[channel-newsletter]] and [[email-components]].
2. Pick the spine. Curated links can follow. They do not replace it.
3. Subject and preview last, as a pair. Open in sentence one.
4. One next step. Human sign-off.
5. Skip the week rather than fill a slot. Cadence is a promise about
   frequency, not a promise to publish filler.
6. [[review]] then hold. `sendReady: no`.

# Output
A filled [[email-components]] pack labelled as an issue, plus what was
cut.

# Guardrails
- Daily hiring scan to Jeremy's work inbox is delivery. Do not rewrite it here.
- Gmail is not connected. Do not pretend a send is possible.
