# Reviewing with fresh eyes

Read [[review]] and the matching channel guide.

Hold the draft to the surface in front of you. Do not hunt for source
material the writer did not hand you. Verdict `ready` or `revise`. Quote
the line, name the rule, give one fix. When torn, choose `revise`. Do
not rewrite the piece.
