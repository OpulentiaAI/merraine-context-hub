# Spear signal vocabulary

## The play

Jeremy configured Spear for maximum surface area, then filter later.

- 33 of 38 triggers on. The five off were the ones that said **Requires Setup**,
  not ones he rejected.
- Engagement over firmographics: who reacted, reposted, or commented on
  LinkedIn, X, and Reddit.
- One 28-keyword set pasted everywhere. The thesis is funding stage plus
  seniority: talent acquisition, executive search, recruitment firm, venture
  backed, growth stage, C-suite recruiting, PE-backed, SaaS hiring, leadership
  hiring, scale-up, Series A/B/C hiring, post-Series A growth, startup
  scaling, early stage recruiting, growth equity, pre-IPO hiring, late stage
  startup, post-acquisition hiring, portfolio company talent, operator hiring,
  VP level recruiting, C-suite hiring.
- Influencer watch: Gary Vaynerchuk, Kass Lazerow, and Mike Lazerow.
- Company watch: **Merraine Group Inc** only. Who touches Merraine content is
  inbound.
- News trigger left without a fixed blast message. One note per person.
- Podcast guest and podcast host both on, same keywords.

X and Reddit keyword triggers were over the keyword limit when this was read.
Do not silently cut the list. Ask before trimming.
