# Use the words buyers already use

# The play
Ask about the last win and the last loss, not a persona. Read public
posts for the verb they use for the job. Quote it. Compliments are noise.
Hypothetical willingness to buy is not evidence.

# How we apply it for Merraine
[[customer-language-first]] plus mailbox extraction, once Gmail is
on. Until then, [[jeremy-voice]] is how *he* writes, not how buyers write.
Do not mix the two.
