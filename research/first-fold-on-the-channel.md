# First fold on the channel

# The play
Email: first 35 to 40 characters of the subject, plus the first body line. LinkedIn: about 140 characters on a phone before "see more". Those are truncation limits, not open-rate targets.

# How we apply it for Merraine
[[shaping-for-the-channel]] rewrites to the fold. [[channel-email]] and [[channel-linkedin]] hold the numbers. Do not quote them as current client measurements without checking.
