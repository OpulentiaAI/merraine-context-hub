# Target conversations, not profiles

# The play
Stop asking "who fits the ICP" and start asking "who is already talking about our category."
Hunt people engaging with the category, commenting on the posts that matter,
and interacting with competitors. Then enter the conversation. Timing beats
volume. Wispra's claim: the more they refined signals, the better the quality
got.

# How we apply it for Merraine
This is [[category-conversation]] and [[posted-hiring-pain]]. The daily
digest already prefers a cited conversation over a static Spear row. Do not
restate their demo math as Merraine's.
