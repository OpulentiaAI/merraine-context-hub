# Pointer or whole thing

# The play
A pointer exists to earn the click: one idea, enough context, the link, before any scroll. A whole thing is the deliverable and has nothing to click through to. The accidental third shape, two thirds of a page plus "read the rest", spends attention and still asks them to leave.

# How we apply it for Merraine
[[adapting-for-email]] picks the shape before it cuts. First touches are pointers. A congratulations note is a whole thing. Cite NN/g for the unread finding, not as our result.
