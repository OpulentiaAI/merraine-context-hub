# Writing an announcement

Read [[announcement]] and [[channel-announcement]].

Lead with the change. Four parts on a public note. Versioned records use
Breaking / New / Improved / Fixed, newest first, dated. No journey
paragraph. No marketing adjectives. `sendReady: no`.
