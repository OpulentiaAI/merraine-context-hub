# LinkedIn

# Shape
Hook, turn, payoff, close. The first line is the whole budget on a phone.
One idea. Deliver the value in the feed or in the DM. Do not make them
leave to get the point. Connection notes stay shorter than a post.
See [[writing-in-linkedin-style]] and [[shaping-for-the-channel]].

# Limits
- About 140 characters show on mobile before "see more". Desktop about 210.
  Those folds are the practical hook budget, not an ideal length.
- A status update caps at 3,000 characters. We never need that.
- Connection note: short, cited, one question. No product name.
- At most one emoji, and only if it earns it. Jeremy almost never uses one.
- Links in a post suppress reach. Put a needed link in the first comment.

# Failures
Paragraph walls. "A few thoughts on…". "I'm humbled to announce".
"Thoughts? 👇". Three questions. Our firm name in a connection request.
A compliment with no object.
