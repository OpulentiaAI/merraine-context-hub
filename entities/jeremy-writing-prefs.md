# Jeremy writing preferences

Standing notes only. A one-off for a single draft does not belong here.

# Standing
- Default surfaces: LinkedIn and email. X, Threads, Bluesky, and Mastodon
  stay off until Jeremy names them.
- Threads stay under 8 posts.
- Sign-off: Best, Jeremy. Cheers when the thread is already warm.
- No em dashes. No firm name in a connection request.
- Propose. Never publish or schedule without the confirm word.
- Long-form lands as a page in Notion when Jeremy wants it kept.

# Do not save
One-off length, a single day's audience, or "make this one sharper".
Those die with the draft.
