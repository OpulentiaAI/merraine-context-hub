# One core message, a few pillars, graded proof

# The play
One core message. Three or four pillars. Proof under each, graded
proven / plausible / assumption. Different leads for different buyers.
Write the objections, including who should not buy.

# How we apply it for Merraine
[[writing-the-message-set]] owns this. Until proof is named, every
claim that needs a logo stays `assumption` and stays out of first touches.
