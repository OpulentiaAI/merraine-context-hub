# Settling positioning

Work in order. What would they do if Merraine did not exist (internal
recruiting, a contingency firm, post-and-see)? What can a search do that
those cannot? Who feels that absence most? The statement comes last.

A differentiator has to be true, provable, and hard to copy. "We care"
and "we are fast" fail the third test.

If two segments pull apart, write the options and the evidence that would
decide. Do not pick one to look finished.

Proof we may cite is still a `[NEED]` on [[icp-context]].
