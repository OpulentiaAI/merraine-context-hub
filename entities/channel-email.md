# Email

# Shape
An email interrupts. A page answers a question someone already had.
Pick **pointer** (one idea, one link, short) or **whole thing** (the
mail is the deliverable). Do not paste two thirds of a page and ask
them to read the rest. See [[adapting-for-email]] and [[email-components]].

# Limits
- Subject hook in the first 35 to 40 characters. Truncation is real.
  An ideal open-rate length is not established.
- Preview typically starts truncating between 35 and 75 characters.
- Body: 80 words for a signal opener, 60 for congratulations.
- One action. Plain text always written.
- Gmail is not connected. Do not pretend a send is possible.

# Failures
Warm-up first line. Subject that hints. Preview left unset. Fake `Re:`.
A merge tag with no fallback (`Hi ,`). A point that lives only in an image.
Hardening a hedged claim while compressing it.
