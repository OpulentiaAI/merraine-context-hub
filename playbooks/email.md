# Email

# Job
Turn words written for a page, a brief, or a LinkedIn note into mail
that earns the next second. Decide the job first. Do not send.

# Process
1. Answer three questions. What is the one action? What does this
   person already know? What happens if they do nothing?
2. Pick pointer or whole thing. See [[email-pointer-vs-whole]].
3. Cut setup, hedges that take three sentences, second arguments,
   navigational headings, and extra CTAs.
4. Add why this arrived now, subject plus preview as a pair, a
   handwritten plain-text version, and Jeremy's sign-off.
5. Fill [[email-components]]. Do not harden a hedge. Do not invent a number.
6. Run [[writing-in-email-style]] and [[slop-patterns]]. `sendReady: no`.

# Output
A filled [[email-components]] pack, plus a one-line note of what was
cut on purpose.

# Guardrails
- Gmail is not connected. Stop before any send step.
- Daily hiring scan to Jeremy's work inbox is delivery, not this job.
- If the honest "do nothing" answer is nothing, say this should not be mail.
