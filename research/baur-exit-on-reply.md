# Automation stops the moment they reply

# The play
Four-step outreach with one hard safeguard: exit on reply. The moment
someone responds, automation stops and a human owns the thread. Signals
(content engagement, views, site behaviour, advocates) prioritise who to
start. The goal is more real conversations, not automated relationships.

# How we apply it for Merraine
[[replies]] and [[unibox-triage]] implement the exit. Jeremy owns
every live thread. Do not keep a sequence running after the first reply.
