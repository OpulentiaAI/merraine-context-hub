# Draft, then hold the clock

# The play
Writing and editing a draft is normal work. Setting a publish time, enabling a campaign, or deleting anything waits for an explicit confirm. Propose the time in the thread. Do not set it.

# How we apply it for Merraine
This is the same gate as `sendReady: no`. [[social-queue]] and [[outreach]] both stop here. The confirm word is still send.
