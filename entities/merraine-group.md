# Merraine Group

A search firm sells placements. Every signal in this hub answers one question:
**who is about to need to hire, and who decides.**

Merraine places senior operators and executives. The buyer is whoever opens the
search: a CEO, COO, CHRO, founder, board member, or PE operating partner. The
product is a shortlist of operators they could not have reached themselves.

East Coast rooted, about 25 years in. Backed by Kass and Mike Lazerow (Buddy
Media). CultureHub Insights joined in March 2026. The Notion workspace already
connected here is named **Shepherd Search Group / Merraine**.

Daily team: [[jeremy-sanchez]], Dina Ufberg (COO and Co-Owner), Codi McCommon,
Reid Lortz, David Gantshar, Jane Nueva.

accounts already in motion live in [[active-accounts]].
